How the research moved

Same day, six steps. The story is not “more experiments = better.” It is: find a paper champion, try to beat it fairly, then stop when knobs stop helping.

The one-sentence arc

A simple fast trend rule won the first screen. Year-by-year tests kept it alive but did not make it a stay-invested rule. Slower copies lost. A crash-exit idea improved, then got parked. Next work should be a new family.

  1. 1 · Screen Keep

    First grids — BTC & ALGO

    Learned: A “short average above long average” rule (5 & 20 days) was the standout on Bitcoin. The same idea also rescued Algorand vs just holding it. Combining trend with an RSI “hot/cold” gauge mostly never traded.

    Paper $1,000 on BTC, Jan 2023 → Sep 2026: $6,196 vs hold $4,619. Worst dip about −18% vs −53%.

  2. 2 · Stress Tweak

    Was the fast rule just one lucky bull?

    Learned: No — it beat hold in 6 of 7 calendar years and lost less in 2022. On shorter rolling windows it only beat hold 13 of 23 times. Useful shock absorber, not an obvious always-winner. It still sells Bitcoin often.

  3. 3 · Stickier Tweak family

    Sticky SMA vs the fast rule

    Learned: Slower defaults (20/50, wait-to-confirm, small exit gap) were supposed to stay invested more and grow more Bitcoin. They roughly matched hold dollars while sitting in cash ~44% of the time, and lost badly to the fast rule. The idea stayed alive for a grid search.

    Open brief →
  4. 4 · Grid Tweak → Drop

    81 sticky settings later

    Learned: Knobs helped the defaults. The best sticky still ended with about 0.22 BTC units vs 0.61 for the fast rule on the same 2020–2026 paper path. Year-split vs hold stayed 3/7 (fast is 6/7). Soft dips are not enough if you miss the bull years. Drop sticky as a Bitcoin rule.

  5. 5 · Crash exit Tweak family

    Hold-protect defaults

    Learned: “Stay in, leave only on a deep drop, climb back in” is the right shape for a stay-invested idea. The first settings (25% drop / 10% bounce) softened the crash (−28% vs hold −76%) but grew fewer Bitcoin units than holding. Fast SMA still dominated. Wider re-entry looked promising.

    Open brief →
  6. 6 · Park Parked

    Hold-protect 20/15, then stop

    Learned: Better knobs (20% drop / 15% bounce) beat hold on dollars and Bitcoin units, and won 5 of 7 years. Still about 3× fewer units than the fast trend rule. The family is documented. Do not keep searching the same two knobs.
