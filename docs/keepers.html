Keepers — and what that word means

Keep here means “keep as a paper baseline or a reference,” not “go trade it.” Everything on this page used a hypothetical $1,000. Spot-style ideas, no leverage, no live orders.

If you only remember three things: Donchian 10/5 is the alts KEEP (extreme paper/historical $ — not a live promise). Fast SMA 5/20 is still the Bitcoin paper bar. Buy-and-hold is the honest default on each coin. Nothing is a live order.

6 Sep 2026 Keep · alts leader

Donchian breakout 10/5 (ALGO + ETH)

Buy a fresh 10-day high, sell a 5-day low. This is the family that landed after the Bitcoin knob search parked. On the published alts screen it beat hold every year and held up out of sample. Paper-live books are now watching it — that is a forward clock, not a new KEEP.

Those million-dollar figures are compounded paper/historical paths over ~7 years of crypto — not a forecast and not a live account. Treat them as “the backtest was unusually strong,” then read the paper-live card for the $1,000 books that actually start now.

6 Sep 2026 Keep · BTC paper bar

Fast trend rule (5-day vs 20-day average)

Be invested in Bitcoin when the short average is above the long one; otherwise cash. On every Bitcoin comparison so far, this printed more paper dollars and more Bitcoin units than the alternatives.

First screen end$1,000 → $6,196
Same period, just hold$4,619
Worst dip (screen)about −18%
Years vs hold6 / 7 beat

Why it is still only a Bitcoin paper keep: it jumps in and out. On shorter rolling tests it beat hold only a bit more than half the time. On alts, Donchian later beat this same SMA idea. Treat Fast SMA as the BTC scoreboard, not a green light.

Keep · reference

Buy & hold Bitcoin

Buy once, never time it. Always invested. This is the honest default: if a clever rule cannot beat “do nothing,” it is not clever.

The lab also counts ending Bitcoin units — how much BTC the paper account would still hold — so a rule cannot “win” by sitting in cash through a bull market and looking fine in dollars only.

Parked · documented

Hold-protect 20/15 (not a champion)

Stay in Bitcoin, leave after a ~20% drop from a peak, climb back after a 15% bounce. This is the first crash-exit recipe that grew more paper BTC units than holding (0.21 vs 0.13 on the 2020–2026 path). It is still far behind the fast trend rule (0.61 units).

Parked means: we wrote it down so we do not re-search the same two knobs. It is not a drop (the idea has a shape), and it is not a keep (it lost the paper pile race).

Tweak · not a keeper

Using the fast rule as if it were live

Same SMA 5/20 idea, different question: “should this replace just holding?” Year-by-year it often wins and it softens crashes — but it sells Bitcoin regularly. That stays a tweak. Research may continue; live use is not the verdict.

What did not survive

  • Drop   Sticky SMA defaults — matched hold dollars, missed bulls, lost to the fast rule.
  • Drop   Sticky SMA as a Bitcoin rule after 81-setting grid — knobs helped, family still not close.
  • Drop   Hold-protect defaults (25/10) — fewer BTC units than holding.
