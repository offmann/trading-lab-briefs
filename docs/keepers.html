Keepers — and what that word means

Keep here means “keep as a paper baseline or a reference,” not “go trade it.” Everything on this page used a hypothetical $1,000. Spot-style ideas, no leverage, no live orders. The 9 Sep revalidation is the scorecard; older KEEP chips on individual briefs are history.

If you only remember two survivors: buy&hold Bitcoin is the paper bar ($1,000 → $19,879, dip −76%). Donchian 20/10 on ALGO is the only active KEEP ($1,000 → $10,663 vs hold $79). Donchian 10/5 and Fast SMA are demoted. Nothing is a live order.
