What we tested

plain English
  • We compared simple rules for when to be invested in Bitcoin (BTC-USD) and Algorand (ALGO-USD) vs sitting in cash.
  • Period: Jan 1, 2023 → Sep 5, 2026, using one price per day (not minute-by-minute).
  • Costs: we subtracted trading fees so results are a bit pessimistic.

what happened

BASELINE

2. Winner — short avg above long avg

WINNER

Rule: be invested when the 5-day average is above the 20-day average (trend up); otherwise cash. On BTC this was the standout.

Same rule on ALGO: $4290 — a huge upgrade vs holding ALGO ($567).
