Dead on alts

trend_rsi on alts — 0 trades across all 36 grid configs on ALGO. The "buy pullbacks in an uptrend" logic never fires because alts don't pull back gently — they crash or spike. RSI conditions are too restrictive for volatile coins.
Dropped
vol_filter_sma on alts — at low ATR thresholds (1.5–2.5%) it's identical to plain SMA crossover (crypto is always volatile, the gate never closes). At high thresholds (4%) it kills good entries too. The concept makes sense for equities but adds zero value on crypto.

Five things learned

  • Donchian breakout is naturally suited to crypto alts — the "buy new highs, cut at new lows" logic catches crypto's asymmetric rallies (10–50× up, then −90% crash) without needing any indicator tuning.
  • ALGO buy-and-hold is −92% since 2019 — this is the harshest baseline in the repo. Almost anything that avoids the worst crashes "beats hold" on ALGO, but Donchian does it with real conviction (8/8 years, 96% WF folds).
  • Trend-RSI is an honest null — the "dip in uptrend" concept requires a clean uptrend with orderly pullbacks. Alts don't do orderly. 36 grid configs, zero entries. This is useful negative knowledge.
  • Vol filtering is pointless on crypto — crypto ATR/price is always above 1–3%. The gate never engages. You'd need a fundamentally different chop filter for crypto (e.g. regime detection).
  • 7-year compounding can produce extreme numbers — $8.8 million from $1,000 sounds absurd, but ALGO itself went 80× in 2021. Donchian was long for most of that move and dodged the 2022 crash. The compounding is real but the opportunity was historically unusual.
