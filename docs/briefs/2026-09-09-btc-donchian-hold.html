Slower Donchian exits sit longer in BTC — and give back a lot of dollars

Idea: keep the same “buy the breakout” rule as Donchian 10/5 (price makes a new 10-day high), but sell less often — wait for a slower low, or require a deeper dip below that low. That matches a stay-invested BTC posture better than flipping 88 times. Result: time in the market does go up (43% → 60–77%). Ending coin-units and dollars do not — they fall vs 10/5. Family is Tweak, not a new Keep. Donchian 10/5 stays the BTC paper Keep.

What the rule is, in one sentence

donchian_hold buys when today’s close breaks above the prior N-day high (same as Donchian). It sells only when the close falls through a slower M-day low, optionally by an extra gap. Optional “confirm” waits extra days above the high before buying.

  • entry_period — lookback for the buy high (kept at 10, same as the Keep).
  • exit_period — lookback for the sell low (10 / 15 / 20 vs Donchian’s 5).
  • confirm_bars — extra days the breakout must last (1 = no delay).
  • exit_gap_pct — extra % below the low before selling (0 = plain channel).

Small grid: 12 combos. Sacred harness unchanged — we did not cheapen fees to look good.
