SMA 5/20 on Bitcoin: softens crashes, still sells too often

We stress-tested the short/fast moving-average rule (5 & 20 days) year by year and in rolling 90-day windows. It often beats “just hold BTC” on paper — especially in ugly years — but it regularly steps out of Bitcoin. For your stay-invested style, that’s a tweak signal, not a green light.
