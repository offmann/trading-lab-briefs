## Verdict: **TWEAK** (not Keep, not Drop)

SMA 5/20 on BTC **often beats buy-and-hold on a calendar-year basis** and **softens bad years**, but it still sells BTC regularly. If the goal is stay invested most of the time, a rule that flips in and out of BTC needs a clearer “obvious sell” bar before it could graduate from paper. Keep researching; don’t treat this as a live replacement for holding BTC.
