This brief supersedes earlier Donchian and SMA paper dollars (BTC transfer, alts screen, Donchian-hold hybrid). Secondary · Donchian-hold grid on BTC

Same 12-cell exit-stickiness grid as the Sep 9 hybrid brief, re-run on the fixed engine. The old “winner” (10/10, confirm=1, gap=0) now loses to hold ($10.4k vs $19.9k; 2/8 years; WF 11/27).

Honest-engine $ / Sharpe winner is entry=10, exit=15, confirm=2, gap=0: $26,148, units 0.33 vs hold 0.25, dip −46% vs −76%, 26 trades. Year-split 4/8. Walk-forward still only 11/27 (41%).

TWEAK, not KEEP. Full sample can beat hold with slower exits + a 2-bar confirm. Out-of-sample does not. Also: confirm_bars=2 is no longer the “trap” the old engine claimed — that was look-ahead talking. Do not promote a 12-cell winner that fails walk-forward.
