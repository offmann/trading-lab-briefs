Five insights

  • Year-split 1/7 → 5/7 with 20/15 — knobs mattered; defaults were wrong, not the family.
  • Ending units 0.210 > B&H 0.132 — first hold-protect config that grows the BTC pile vs hold.
  • Still ≪ SMA 5/20 units 0.612 — soft-hold recipe, not the paper alpha winner.
  • WF beat-hold 43% (SMA 57%, defaults 26%); mean return actually highest (+14.3%).
  • Time-in-market 54.7% ≈ SMA; trades 15 vs 70 — calmer, closer to core style.
