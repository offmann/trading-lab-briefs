Tweak

Fast trend as a “live-style” rule

It often beats hold and softens ugly years, but it sells Bitcoin regularly. That is a tweak, not a keep, if the point is to stay invested most of the time.

Tweak

Hold-protect 20/15 — soft-hold recipe

Stay in, exit after a ~20% drop, climb back in after a 15% bounce. Beats hold on the long paper path. Still far behind the fast trend rule. Documented as a softer option worth remembering, not crowned.

Parked

Hold-protect family on Bitcoin

The knobs were searched. Stop spinning the same dials. Optional later branch: one Fear & Greed experiment — treat that as new work, not more crash-exit grids.

Why both? The 20/15 recipe above is the best version found — worth a note. This "Parked" label means stop searching for even more knob combinations on the same crash-exit idea.

Drop

Sticky SMA defaults, and sticky as a BTC rule

Meant to stay invested longer. On paper it roughly matched hold while sitting in cash ~44% of the time, and lost to the fast rule. A grid helped the defaults — still not close.

Drop

Trend + RSI combo, and hold-protect defaults
